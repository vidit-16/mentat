Vidit Choudhary

The model the site runs: what it is, what it was trained on, how it scores, and its limits.

The model

WhatThree linear classifiers (intent: 96 classes, flow: 10, action: 30) and a confidence model
Trained onreference/train.jsonl.gz, sha256 9243c4129368a7696526688990edde9ad0243f842f987ec5f1196bfdb89011be, plus 3962 noisy copies made by src/triage/train.py
Never trained ondev.jsonl, dev_noisy.jsonl, hostile.jsonl, or any held-out data
Training commandscripts/reproduce.sh train (run id train-003, about 6 minutes on a 4-core laptop; 4.6 minutes on a 2-vCPU GitHub Actions runner)
Seed20261117
Librariesnumpy 2.5.5, scikit-learn 1.9.1 (training only)
Features93,441 sparse features in blocks w, c, s, x (see src/triage/features.py)
Temperaturesaction 0.33218, flow 0.35914, intent 0.22484
Filesmodel.npz (weights as float16, no pickle), vocab.json, meta.json, confidence.json
Versionfirst 16 hex of sha256 over the four files; shown by /healthz and in every response

Out-of-fold results on the training data (5 folds grouped by conversation): intent 0.7097, action after the policy gate 0.8211, top-70% error ratio 0.3076, routing threshold 0.5663 which automates 84.7% of messages at 90% accuracy.

train-003 differs from train-002 only in the confidence model: unseen_words is now measured out of fold, as the service measures it (experiment log, 20 November). The three classifier heads are byte for byte the same.

confidence.json also holds the confidence caps applied after the model (policy conflict, injection pattern, odd characters, little context). The little_context cap was added after training (experiment log, 17 November, hostile-002); retraining writes it by default.

Known limits: sees only the last 8 turns; trained on one synthetic retailer's procedures; weak on the next step at procedure branch points.
